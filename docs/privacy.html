DotaRazbor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

DotaRazbor is a free Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App (@DotaRazbor_bot) that analyzes Dota 2 matches. This policy explains what data it uses and how. DotaRazbor is run by an independent developer.

Data we store

  • Your Telegram user ID and the Dota 2 account ID you link to the bot, so it can find your matches.
  • A daily counter of AI analyses per Telegram user ID, to enforce usage limits.

We do not store your name, phone number, messages, payment data or contacts. We do not sell or share stored data with anyone.

Data we process but do not store

  • Public match data from the OpenDota API (match statistics, items, heroes) for the matches you ask about.
  • When you request an AI analysis, a summary of match statistics (no personal data) is sent to an AI provider (Groq or Google Gemini) to generate advice. The request passes through our proxy, which keeps short-lived request counters per IP address (deleted automatically within 2 days) only to limit abuse.

YouTube API Services

We use YouTube API Services only to upload our own promotional videos to our own YouTube channel. Our API client does not access, collect, store or display any data of YouTube users, and no third party signs in to it. By watching our videos on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to our client can be revoked at Google security settings.

Cookies

The Mini App does not use cookies or tracking. It may cache app files and public game reference data on your device to load faster.

Your choices

You can unlink your Dota 2 account or ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to @DotaRazbor_bot. We delete it within 30 days.

Terms

Use of DotaRazbor is also governed by our Terms of Service.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.

Политика конфиденциальности

DotaRazbor — бесплатный Telegram-бот и мини-приложение для разбора матчей Dota 2. Мы храним только ваш Telegram ID, привязанный ID аккаунта Dota 2 и счётчик ИИ-разборов за день. Статистику матчей берём из открытого OpenDota API. Для ИИ-разбора сводка статистики без личных данных уходит провайдеру ИИ (Groq или Google Gemini). Сервисы YouTube API используются только для загрузки наших роликов на наш канал, данные пользователей YouTube мы не получаем. Данные никому не продаём и не передаём. Чтобы удалить свои данные, напишите боту @DotaRazbor_bot.
